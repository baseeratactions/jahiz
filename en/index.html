How does Jahiz work?

Jahiz is an intermediary: we list goods and services and take the orders, then forward them to our accredited partner companies who fulfil and deliver them. Each partner pays us an agreed commission, so you pay no extra brokerage fee.

Common questions

Does Jahiz sell the products itself?

Jahiz is an intermediary: we list products and services, take your order and follow it, while accredited companies and shops prepare, fulfil and deliver it. We stay your point of contact until the order arrives.

Do I pay Jahiz a fee?

No. You pay the price of the product or service and any delivery fee; Jahiz earns its commission from the fulfilling partner as agreed between them.

Why are prices shown as “from”?

Prices vary by brand, quantity, area and partner. We confirm the final price before fulfilment and only proceed with your approval.

How do I pay?

Cash or card on delivery, or bank transfer for large and company orders.

What if a product is wrong or a service is late?

Message us on WhatsApp straight away. We follow up with the partner to replace the product, redo the service or refund under the partner's policy, and record it in their rating.

How do you choose accredited partners?

We check the commercial registration, licences and health certificates, try the service, and keep following customer ratings. Partners whose rating drops stop receiving orders.

Can companies order in bulk?

Yes. Choose “Company” as the customer type and write the quantities in the notes; we send you quotes from more than one partner.

I am a merchant. How do I join?

Fill in the partner application, and our partnerships team will contact you to review your licences and agree the commission and how orders reach you.
