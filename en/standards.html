Our commitment

Our service standards

We stay your single point of contact from the moment you order until it reaches you. These are the principles we follow on every order.

What we commit to on every order

  • We confirm the price, time, address and payment method with you before any order is fulfilled.
  • Your order goes only to a licensed partner company we have approved.
  • We aim to reply to every order within one working day.
  • We follow fulfilment and delivery, and tell you about any delay and its reason before you need to ask.
  • We share with the fulfilling partner only the details needed for delivery.
  • A partner with repeated shortcomings stops receiving orders.
