About Jahiz

We connect customers with accredited companies, and follow every order until it arrives

Jahiz is an intermediary company in Qatar specialised in selling goods, supplies and services through social media. We make buying and shopping easier for customers, and open a new channel of orders for merchants and service providers without marketing costs.

Our model

We listWe list and market goods and services on social media and on this website.
We take the orderWe receive the customer's order and confirm price, timing, address and payment.
We forward itWe send the order to an accredited partner to fulfil and deliver, and follow the quality.
We earn a commissionOur income comes from accredited partners at rates and fees agreed in advance in the partnership contract.

What each side gets

Customers

  • Food, supplies and services in one place
  • Easy ordering on the site or by social media message
  • Accredited partners and follow-up until delivery
  • No brokerage fee for customers

Jahiz

  • Listings, marketing and social media accounts
  • Accrediting partners and checking their licences
  • Receiving, routing and following orders
  • Customer service, problem solving and commission settlement

Accredited partners

  • Ready-to-fulfil orders without marketing costs
  • Commission on fulfilled orders only
  • Monthly statement, order and rating reports
  • A long-term partnership that grows with volume

Principles we work by

  • We only accredit licensed companies we trust
  • We always confirm the price with the customer first
  • Commission is clear and written in every partner contract
  • Customer data is shared with the partner only as needed for delivery
  • We stay the customer's point of contact until the order arrives
  • Partners whose rating drops stop receiving orders
